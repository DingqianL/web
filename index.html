About

Dingqian (Sara) Liu is an Economist turned Quantitative Researcher at American Century Investments. Her work sits at the intersection of financial economics, macroeconomics, and data science.


She is currently engaged in research on firm-level financing decisions, the measurement of managerial attention, and the effects of economic policy uncertainty on financial markets and growth. Sara was born and raised in Sichuan, China and holds a B.A. in Finance from the University of International Business and Economics (UIBE).
